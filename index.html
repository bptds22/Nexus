How it works

From high school to CEGEP in 3 steps

Welead makes the path from high school athlete to college prospect simple, transparent, and data-driven.

1
🏫

Coaches Add Athletes

High school coaches register their athletes on Welead. Each player gets a profile with academic info, sport stats, and video uploads. Verified by the school.

2
⚡

Athletes Showcase Talent

Athletes build out their profiles with highlights, references, and personal bios. Their stats update automatically each season, keeping the profile fresh and relevant.

3
🎯

Recruiters Discover Prospects

CEGEP coaches search, filter, and bookmark athletes that fit their program. They reach out directly through the platform — no middlemen, no friction.
